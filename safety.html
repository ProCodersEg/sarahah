App Safety Standards

Content Moderation

We are committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment in our public chat rooms and private messages. Our moderation system includes:

  • Automated filters to detect and block inappropriate language or harmful content.
  • Active moderation by a team of professionals to review reported content.
  • Immediate removal of content that violates our community guidelines.

User Verification

To enhance safety, we require all users to register using a valid email address. Additionally, suspicious accounts may be flagged for verification to ensure authenticity.

Reporting and Blocking

We provide tools for users to report inappropriate behavior or block unwanted interactions. Reports are reviewed promptly, and appropriate action is taken, including account suspension or banning if necessary.

Parental Supervision

For younger users, we recommend parental guidance when using the app. Parents can:

  • Monitor app activity through shared accounts or supervised modes.
  • Encourage open discussions about online safety and responsible communication.

Data Privacy

We prioritize the protection of user data, especially for minors. Our privacy measures include:

  • Secure encryption of all messages and personal data.
  • Adherence to GDPR, COPPA, and other relevant data protection regulations.
  • Clear opt-in permissions for data collection, with no data sharing to third parties without consent.

For more details, refer to our Privacy Policy.
